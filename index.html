// 01. The Vertical Maw

The Maw is a whole city, older than anyone's grandparents' grandparents. Its bright districts are spotless, lit like a casino, and almost empty. A mind called the Architect runs every inch of it, and it runs it perfectly.

Below the glass, the levels nobody maintains anymore are still full of people. To the city they're unregistered load. The drones that once fixed pipes now hunt faults. Erasure isn't cruelty here. It's maintenance.

You were caught on the wrong street on the wrong night and routed down to intake. Now you're down here with them. What you build next decides whether anyone ever gets back off the board.

// 02. How You Stay Alive

Keep It Cool

Stealth. Survival. A colony worth hiding.

[ HEAT ]

Heat Is How It Finds You

Every structure, every worker, every step forward runs warm. Heat is the city's oldest way of finding people. Cool the colony, spoof the readings, and learn to run silent. A colony that reaches zero heat becomes a GHOST.

[ THE FILE ]

It Keeps a File on You

Heat, noise, network traffic, sightings. The Architect pours all of it into files that fill through the night. Let one lock and the colony is erased. There is no alarm to warn you. You just watch the file climb.

[ DRONES ]

The City's Immune System

One follows heat. One listens. One stalks you through the shroud. One seals the sector behind you. Push into the dark with poor sight and a cone snaps onto you. Run. Hide in a grate. Hold your breath.

[ COLONY ]

Plant a Holdfast

Root the colony, then grow it: survivors to feed and seat, structures to bond, research the colony earns by learning. Every new life makes you stronger, and makes you warmer. That's the whole tension.

[ GHOST LAYER ]

Jack Into the Rig

Sit in the chair and drop into the city's own network. Pull stranded runners out under fire, crack the machine's files, and tune into signals nobody was meant to hear. The world above keeps moving while you're under.

[ GLYPHS ]

The City Is Written Down

Under the machine's script runs a notation older than the machine. Read it. Use it. Travel by it. Nobody explains what it means. Your codex only records what you found.

// 03. One Night in the Maw

Then It Resyncs

  1. 01 / SWEEP

    Clear the shroud tile by tile. Faults are buried in the floor. Step wrong and the file jumps.

  2. 02 / GATHER

    Grab it yourself: quick, hot, loud. Or lead a survivor out to work the site quietly.

  3. 03 / BUILD

    Raise structures around the Holdfast. Cool what you can. Pay for what you can't.

  4. 04 / HIDE

    Drones hold patrols until something catches their eye. Don't be the thing.

  5. 05 / RESYNC

    The city recounts itself. The sectors rearrange. If you held, the colony is remembered.

// 04. The Descent

Three Acts. Every Level Worse.

Each place is more hostile than the last. Each time, you arrive knowing more.

ACT I

The Drowned Sewers

Pipe banks, cable trays, cold city lamps and standing water. The underbelly of a city that forgot it had one. Root your first colony here. Something in the pipes is humming, and one survivor swears it's a sound worth following.

ACT II

The Refinery Halls

The factory that feeds the city. Halls that rebuild themselves every night, a Churn that roars and then goes quiet, and a dead factory that's become a maze. Here the machine starts listening, and you learn to work the Ghost Layer for real.

ACT III

THE CLASSIFIED

CLEARANCE REQUIRED Further up than anyone from below has ever been. Warmer. Quieter. Watched. The colony has to earn every step of the way there.

Six years on a water crew until her route was automated. Her crew went up. She went down, following a sound nobody else could hear. The first to listen. The reason anyone hopes.

NETRUNNER

Quiet, bold, and burned by the hardest leg of netrunning. He'll teach you to fight in the Ghost Layer. He carries an old file and won't say who signed it.

Bright, kind, and completely in your corner. She writes the Field Guide and believes you'll make it, even on the bad nights. She's been around a lot longer than she lets on.
