// 01. Project Focus: HoldFast

Automate. Interconnect. Survive.

In HoldFast, survival isn't just about gathering stockpiles—it's about engineering resilient logistical networks. Every raw resource must be mined, routed, refined, and balanced against strict energy thresholds.

Program autonomous nanobot gathering swarms to scour harsh frontiers while you diagnose and resolve single points of failure before a cascading bottleneck brings down your entire base infrastructure.
