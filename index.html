// 01. The Vertical Maw

A City That
Doesn't Need You

The Vertical Maw is a vast, ancient city that runs itself. Its lights never go out. Its systems never sleep. It watches everything, and it keeps a file on everyone.

Far below the bright districts, the forgotten levels are still home to people the city stopped counting. To the machine, they're an error. The drones down there have one job, and it isn't rescue.

Now you're down here too. Survive the night. Then the next one. Then build something worth surviving for.

// 02. How You Stay Alive

Keep It Cool

Stealth. Survival. A colony worth hiding.

[ HEAT ]

Heat Gives You Away

Every structure, every worker, every step forward runs warm, and the city is always measuring. Cool what you can. Hide what you can't. The better you build, the harder it gets to stay hidden.

[ DRONES ]

Something Is Always Looking

Patrols sweep the dark with cold cones of light. Push into the shroud with poor sight and one turns toward you. Run. Hide. Hold your breath.

[ COLONY ]

Plant a Holdfast

Root your colony and grow it. Take in survivors, feed them, put them to work, research new ways to live. Every new life makes you stronger. Every new life makes you warmer.

[ THE RIG ]

Jack In

Sit in the chair and drop into the city's own network. What you do in there changes what happens out here. Just remember the world doesn't stop while you're under.
